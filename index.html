Research

Three current projects

My research sits where second language acquisition meets psycholinguistics, applied linguistics, and the speech sciences.

Pronunciation Development

How does pronunciation develop, and how can we teach it more effectively?

This research looks at pronunciation from three angles:

  • Development: Spanish pronunciation development over three semesters of study
  • Skills: the roles of cognitive measures, auditory acuity, and speech motor skill, in learners of Spanish and of Hul’q’umi’num’
  • Pronunciation Training: Spanish pronunciation training methods delivered with online asynchronous tools
  • Spanish
  • Hul’q’umi’num’

Multilingual acquisition

How do the languages you already speak shape the next one?

My current project investigates trilingual German–English–Spanish speakers learning an artificial language I created. Earlier work examined how L1 Mandarin–L2 English speakers acquire L3 Spanish and L3 French.

  • German
  • English
  • Spanish
  • Mandarin
  • French

AI and language learning

Can conversation practice with AI conversation agents improve spoken Spanish?

I am studying how conversations with AI agents, which give learners automated feedback, can help students improve their oral production in Spanish.

Teaching and leadership

Leading Spanish language teaching at Florida State

Since 2024 I have directed the Spanish Basic Language Program in the Department of Modern Languages and Linguistics. I design, evaluate, and implement changes to the beginning and intermediate Spanish curriculum, and support the instructors who teach it.

I also supervise graduate students and mentor undergraduate research assistants on my projects.
